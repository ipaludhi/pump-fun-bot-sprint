Grinder-rider: a slow-flow pump.fun paper-trading bot for make.fun's "Pump.fun Bot Sprint: Make Bot And Win" bounty, $2 in USDC. It reads the replay's market events as they arrive and decides when to buy, when to sell and how much to risk — entering coins aged 500–2500 slots on proven flow (real reserves ≥ 8 SOL, ≥ 8 distinct buyers, net inflow ≥ 2 SOL), riding them with a 10% trailing stop armed at +10% and a 15% stop-loss, pyramiding up to 3 adds of 0.25 SOL into strength.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
